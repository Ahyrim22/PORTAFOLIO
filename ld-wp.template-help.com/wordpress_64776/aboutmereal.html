WHO I AM

Hi! I'm Ahyram Martínez – a science enthusiast with a passion for understanding the world.

My name is Ahyram Martínez Núñez, and I am a science student from La Chorrera, Panama. I am particularly interested in understanding how the world works through mathematics, physics, and technology.

For me, learning is not limited to memorizing formulas or following procedures. I want to understand why something works, how its components interact, and what principles explain the results we observe.

My interests extend from mathematical reasoning and calculus to physical phenomena, electronics, programming, and technological innovation.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

I believe education should be an active process. I study mathematics and physics on my own, from calculus to electromagnetism and electrical systems, and I like connecting theoretical physics with practical engineering.

From Knowledge to Application

Through academic projects I have explored renewable energy generation, electrical components, programming, and educational game development. Solving real problems means planning, testing, adapting, documenting, and learning from mistakes.

Personal Development

Discipline, adaptability, responsibility, and perseverance guide me. I value communication and collaboration, and I believe in sharing knowledge, because teaching complex ideas requires understanding them deeply.
